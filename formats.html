Direct Summary

Sverkaus delivers two clearly delineated ML-ready product tiers—Video-Only ML-Ready Datasets (Real2Sim, VLM/world-model pre-training) and Action/Kinematic-Labeled Multimodal Datasets—exported in LeRobot, RLDS, MCAP / Rerun, HDF5 / Zarr, or custom Parquet + MP4. Every batch ships with a signed provenance manifest, SHA-256 episode checksums, per-session camera intrinsics/extrinsics, synced base + wrist subgoals, and zero fabricated robot actions.

02 · Data Integrity & Provenance

Clean Stream Separation & World-Model Conditioning.

Integrity Rule 01

Zero Fabricated Robot Actions

Observation-only human video and physically measured kinematic/action streams (3D hand joints, UMI aperture/pose, MoCap glove angles, contact force) are stored in separate, explicitly typed channels. Human-to-robot retargeting is never silently hallucinated into the raw export.

Integrity Rule 02

World-Model & Subgoal Conditioning

Synchronized base + wrist camera frames paired with structured subtask boundaries (approach, grasp, slip, recovery, completion) allow a single batch to train both action policies and video/world models conditioned on visual subgoals.

Integrity Rule 03

Calibration, Units & SHA-256 Chain

Every batch manifest documents camera intrinsics/extrinsics, coordinate frames, force/pose units, p95 sync drift, known limitations, and per-episode SHA-256 checksums for verifiable chain-of-custody.

Integrity Rule 04

Separate Recorded vs. Accepted Accounting

Recorded, submitted, and accepted hours are logged in distinct fields alongside Edge QA statuses (RELEASE, HOLD, REJECT), consent references, and privacy exclusion/redaction logs.

04 · Format FAQ

Pipeline & Schema Questions.

Which robotics ML dataset formats does Sverkaus export?
Sverkaus exports batches in LeRobot (Parquet + MP4), RLDS (TensorFlow/JAX ecosystem), MCAP / Rerun (synchronized multimodal playback and inspection), HDF5 / Zarr (ACT, Diffusion Policy, and custom PyTorch loaders), or buyer-specified custom Parquet + MP4 schemas.
Does Sverkaus fabricate synthetic robot joint commands from human video?
No. Sverkaus strictly separates observation-only human data from physically measured kinematic, UMI gripper, MoCap glove, or force streams, and documents every stream's exact sensor provenance in the batch manifest. Human-to-robot retargeting is kept as a separately validated stage—never silently injected as fake robot commands.
What is included inside a Sverkaus batch provenance manifest?
Every delivered batch includes a structured JSON provenance manifest containing hardware/sensor specifications, per-session camera intrinsics and extrinsics, p95 timestamp synchronization metrics, SHA-256 episode integrity checksums, separate recorded/submitted/accepted hour totals, task/subtask hierarchy, failure/recovery flags, Edge QA gate status (RELEASE/HOLD/REJECT), and privacy exclusion/redaction logs.
