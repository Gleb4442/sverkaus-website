Workplace Privacy by Design, EU AI Act & GDPR Governance

How Sverkaus protects commercial trade secrets, employee privacy, and shift continuity across active partner facilities in Ukraine, Germany, Poland, and international jurisdictions—with an auditable 6-document legal packet and optional Data-to-Automation path.

Direct Summary

Every Sverkaus workplace capture operates under strict Privacy by Design and Site Security Clearance (GO / HOLD / NO GO): a 6-document legal clearance packet is signed before setup; capture rigs run offline with zero connection to corporate IT networks and zero audio recording by default; camera geometry (Scoped FOV) excludes bystanders, faces, monitors, printed docs, security systems, and license plates; EU projects align with GDPR (DSGVO / RODO) and EU AI Act provenance; and every released batch ships with verifiable consent and exclusion/redaction logs.

03 · Privacy & Partner FAQ

Governance & Compliance Questions.

How does Sverkaus protect commercial trade secrets and sensitive workplace areas during filming?
Before any camera enters a facility, Sverkaus completes Site Security Clearance (GO/HOLD/NO GO) and executes a 6-document legal packet including a Site Access & Data Collection Agreement and NDA. Capture rigs operate offline without connecting to corporate IT networks, audio is disabled by default, and Scoped FOV geometry frames only workbenches, hands, and tools—excluding bystanders, monitors, ERP screens, printed documents, security cameras, license plates, and restricted infrastructure.
How are employee consent, GDPR / DSGVO / RODO, and EU AI Act provenance handled?
Staff participation is strictly voluntary and documented via signed Participant Consent / Release Forms and a Privacy Notice prior to recording. Bystanders are excluded by camera geometry or redacted before any footage leaves local processing. EU operations (Germany, Poland) follow GDPR data minimization, Data Processing Addenda (DPA), and auditable provenance/exclusion logs aligned with EU AI Act training-data governance, with local adaptation (e.g., PDPA, PIPEDA, Privacy Act) for projects outside Europe.
Does participating as a Site Partner disrupt shifts or require our staff to learn ML tools?
No. Capture sessions are planned around regular shift schedules using autonomous stands or portable rigs so production continues uninterrupted. Sverkaus handles setup, SOPs, Edge QA, privacy review, and dataset packaging. Facilities provide authorized workspace access and receive agreed project-based compensation.
What is the optional Data-to-Automation (Automation Opportunity Assessment)?
Sverkaus connects industrial workflows with Physical AI: for interested manufacturing and operational Site Partners, we can optionally prepare a structured preliminary overview mapping repetitive manual bottlenecks observed during approved processes and practical automation readiness considerations—as a separate, zero-obligation path without promising guaranteed robot deployment or ROI before engineering feasibility.
