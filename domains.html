Hard Physical Manipulation Domains & Sensor Mapping

Direct Summary

Standard robotics benchmarks over-index on rigid tabletop pick-and-place while under-representing the physical phenomena that break real deployments: deformable textiles, flexible cables, tiny fasteners, liquids, specular reflections, thermal state changes, and skilled trade tool forces. Sverkaus maps each domain directly to the minimum-sufficient calibrated sensor suite (Track A/B/C), preserves natural errors and recoveries where safe, and never asks workers to stage hazardous failures.

03 · Domain FAQ

Modality & Sourcing Questions.

Why are deformable objects like garments, linen, and flexible packaging so difficult for robotics models?
Deformable materials have near-infinite state spaces, self-occlude during bimanual folding, and behave unpredictably under friction or static cling. Capturing live laundry and soft-goods shifts with synchronized left/right wrist cameras, 3D hand kinematics, and preserved snag/recovery trajectories gives models the exact contact cues that simulators struggle to reproduce.
When does Sverkaus add an LWIR thermal / IR layer or tactile/sEMG sensors to a capture rig?
Optional LWIR thermal imaging is added for tasks involving heat transfer, hot cookware, commercial kitchen prep, soldering, liquid pouring, or reflective/transparent surfaces (glass, stainless steel, clear containers) where standard RGB fails. Tactile/force gloves or sEMG are added when contact pressure, surface compliance, or insertion friction cannot be inferred from video alone (as demonstrated in FeelTheForce, arXiv:2506.01944).
Can Sverkaus source skilled trades and custom industrial verticals beyond standard lab benchmarks?
Yes. Sverkaus operates a specification-driven sourcing model. Alongside laundries, electronics/precision repair workshops, commercial kitchens, and 3PL warehouses, we qualify and clear skilled trades and light-manufacturing workflows—including drywall and surface finishing, tiling, plumbing/HVAC/electrical assembly, wire harnesses, and hospitality operations—in Ukraine, Germany, Poland, and additional regions per project.
