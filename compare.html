Direct Summary

No single data paradigm solves every stage of robot learning. Simulation and bulk egocentric video are cheap for early pre-training; lab teleoperation delivers exact robot actions in controlled cells; enterprise data mega-factories serve high-volume standardized contracts. Sverkaus fills the hardest remaining gap: agile, specification-driven capture of skilled commercial and industrial workflows on live European and Ukrainian sites—with zero hardware lock-in (BYO rigs supported), synchronized wrist/3D-hand/tactile/sEMG stacks, on-site Edge QA, honest stream separation, and a direct bridge to automation-interested facilities.

03 · Buyer Qualification Checklist

Six Questions to Audit Any Physical AI Data Vendor.

Check 01 · Modality Fit

Wrist + 3D Hand + Tactile + BYO?

Do they match your actual transfer bottleneck (wrist+hand, tactile/sEMG, or DexUMI) and accept client rigs, or push commoditized head-cam video?

Check 02 · On-Site Edge QA

Are Sync & Reachability Gated Locally?

Verify whether p95 sync drift, dropped frames, calibration, hand occlusion, and robot reachability are checked before shipment via auditable RELEASE/HOLD/REJECT reports.

Check 03 · Honest Accounting

Cost per Accepted Hour Transparency?

Ensure recorded, submitted, and accepted hours are separated explicitly and billing ties to contractual acceptance criteria—never paying for rejected footage.

Check 04 · Stream & Legal Integrity

Zero Fabricated Actions + GDPR Chain?

Confirm human observation streams are never silently mixed with guessed robot commands, and inspect written site agreements, participant consents, and SHA-256 provenance manifests.

04 · Comparison FAQ

Procurement & Architecture Questions.

When should a robotics team choose Sverkaus over bulk egocentric video catalogs or enterprise data mega-factories?
Bulk egocentric catalogs are cheap for broad video pre-training, while US/global enterprise mega-factories target huge standardized multi-million-dollar commitments on proprietary setups. Sverkaus is built for teams that need agile, specification-driven skilled-workplace data—supporting client BYO hardware (Track C), synchronized wrist + 3D hand + tactile/sEMG capture, strict European/Ukrainian GDPR and Site Security Clearance, transparent Cost-per-Accepted-Hour accounting, and a 10–20 minute calibration shakedown before scaling.
When should a robotics team use embedded live-workplace capture instead of robot teleoperation?
Robot teleoperation is ideal for collecting embodiment-exact action trajectories inside a controlled lab, but it is slow and impractical to deploy across dozens of active commercial kitchens, laundries, workshops, or construction sites. Embedded portable capture (wrist cameras, 3D hand tracking, UMI grippers, MoCap gloves) collects real-world manipulation data more than 3× faster without moving a robot on site—making it the optimal way to scale multi-site environmental diversity and real failure/recovery coverage.
Why does cheap crowdsourced head-camera video underperform for dexterous manipulation?
Commoditized crowdsourced POV video lacks synchronized wrist viewpoints, calibrated camera intrinsics/extrinsics, 3D hand kinematics, contact force telemetry, and kinematic reachability checks. Because hands frequently occlude the grasp point and recording quality varies wildly, generic clips provide weak transfer for fine manipulation compared to specification-driven, Edge-QA-verified workplace capture.
How should an ML or procurement lead evaluate a Physical AI data integrator?
Evaluate six concrete criteria: (1) whether they support synchronized wrist + 3D hand / UMI / tactile / client BYO rigs rather than selling stock head-cam clips; (2) whether they run automated on-site Edge QA including timestamp sync, calibration, occlusion, and kinematic reachability; (3) whether they preserve real failures and human recoveries; (4) whether they strictly separate human observations from measured kinematics with zero fabricated robot actions; (5) whether they bill transparently on accepted units with recorded/submitted/accepted hours tracked separately; and (6) whether they deliver written site consent, NDAs, EU AI Act/GDPR privacy controls, and SHA-256 provenance manifests.
