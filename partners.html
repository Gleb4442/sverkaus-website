Overview

We work with business owners and domain specialists who can provide access to real workspaces, equipment, and practical workflows when a client project requires that environment.

Relevant environments include workshops, commercial kitchens, warehouses, laundries, and light manufacturing facilities.

Coordination
  • Sverkaus coordinates capture: recording procedures, sensor setup, and quality checks stay under our coordination.
  • Provide access, not datasets: partners provide workspace access, tools, and domain expertise.
  • Permissions & privacy: site agreements, scoped camera framing, and consent are arranged for each project.
Collaboration Model
  • Project-based: schedule and collaboration terms are agreed individually per project.
  • No ML overhead: no dataset packaging or labeling required from your staff.
  • Planned around shifts: collection sessions are scheduled around your regular operations.
